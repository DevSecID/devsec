About DevSec Indonesia

DevSec Indonesia is a cybersecurity company focused on protecting digital assets through penetration testing, secure development consulting, and structured training programs. We combine deep technical expertise with real-world experience to deliver practical security solutions for businesses and individuals.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted cybersecurity partner in Indonesia, enabling organizations to operate safely in the digital age.

Our Mission

Deliver high-quality security assessments and practical training that bridge the gap between theory and real-world threats.
