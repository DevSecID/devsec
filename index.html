Company Profile

Protecting Indonesia's Digital Future

DevSec Indonesia is a dedicated cybersecurity company founded with a clear mission: to protect organizations and empower professionals through expert services and practical, hands-on training.

Our Vision

To become Indonesia's most trusted cybersecurity partner, enabling businesses and government institutions to operate securely in an increasingly complex and hostile digital landscape.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality security assessments, secure development consulting, and practical training programs that bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world cybersecurity challenges.

Our Story

2018 — DevSec Indonesia was founded by a passionate security researcher who saw the growing need for accessible, high-quality cybersecurity services in Indonesia. What started as a one-person operation conducting freelance penetration tests has evolved into a full-service cybersecurity company.

2020 — We launched our first structured training program to address the critical shortage of cybersecurity talent in the region. The program was an immediate success, attracting professionals from various industries.

2022 — Expanded our service offerings to include incident response, cloud security, and advanced red team exercises. Built partnerships with leading technology companies and educational institutions.

2026 — Today, we have completed over 150 security projects, trained more than 500 professionals, and grown into a team of certified experts committed to strengthening Indonesia's cybersecurity posture.

Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about our services, training programs, and how we work with clients.

We offer a comprehensive range of penetration testing services including:

  • Network infrastructure penetration testing (internal and external)
  • Web application security assessment (OWASP Top 10 coverage)
  • Mobile application testing (iOS and Android)
  • API security testing (REST, GraphQL, SOAP)
  • Cloud configuration review (AWS, GCP, Azure)
  • Social engineering simulation (phishing, vishing, physical)
  • Red team exercises (full-scale adversary simulation)

Each engagement is scoped and tailored to your organization's specific needs, threat landscape, and compliance requirements.

All training programs are delivered live online through video conferencing platforms (Zoom/Google Meet). Each program is structured into three levels:

  • Beginner: Foundational concepts, no prior cybersecurity experience needed (basic IT knowledge recommended).
  • Intermediate: Builds on fundamentals, introduces practical tools and techniques.
  • Advanced: Expert-level content, covers complex scenarios and specialized skills.

Sessions are interactive with live demonstrations. Between sessions, you have access to virtual lab environments for hands-on practice. All sessions are recorded and shared for review.

Prerequisites vary by program and level. Here is a general overview:

  • Malware Development: Beginner requires basic IT knowledge. Intermediate needs programming concepts. Advanced requires OS internals and C/C++ knowledge.
  • Red Team Operations: Beginner requires basic networking & Linux command line. Intermediate needs familiarity with pentesting tools. Advanced expects real engagement experience.
  • Blue Team Defense: Beginner requires basic security concepts. Intermediate needs understanding of network protocols. Advanced expects SOC or security operations experience.
  • Advanced Exploitation: Beginner requires programming (Python/C). Intermediate needs assembly language & debugging. Advanced requires prior exploit development experience.

If you are unsure which level is right for you, contact us for a free consultation to assess your current skills.

Engagement timelines depend on the scope, complexity, and size of the target environment. Typical timeframes are:

  • Web Application Assessment: 1-2 weeks for a typical web application.
  • Network Penetration Testing: 2-3 weeks for a mid-sized internal and external network.
  • Mobile Application Testing: 1-2 weeks per platform (iOS/Android).
  • Full-Scale Red Team Exercise: 4-8 weeks, including reconnaissance, execution, and reporting phases.

We will provide a detailed project timeline during the scoping phase based on your specific environment and requirements.

Confidentiality is fundamental to our operations. All engagements are protected by:

  • Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA): Signed before any work begins.
  • Encryption: All testing data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
  • Secure Report Delivery: Reports are delivered through encrypted channels with access controls.
  • Data Retention Policy: Client data is securely purged after the agreed retention period (typically 90 days after project completion), unless otherwise contractually specified.
  • Access Control: Only authorized team members working on your engagement have access to your data.
