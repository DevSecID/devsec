Company Profile

Who We Are

DevSec Indonesia is a dedicated cybersecurity company focused on protecting organizations and empowering professionals through expert services and hands-on training programs.

Our Vision

To become Indonesia's most trusted cybersecurity partner, enabling businesses and government institutions to operate securely in an increasingly complex digital landscape.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality security assessments, secure development consulting, and practical training that bridges the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world cybersecurity challenges.

Our Story

Founded in 2018, DevSec Indonesia started as a small team of security researchers passionate about protecting digital assets. What began as freelance penetration testing has grown into a full-service cybersecurity company serving enterprises, startups, and government agencies across Indonesia.

Over the years, we have completed more than 150 security assessment projects, trained over 500 professionals through our structured programs, and built partnerships with leading technology companies and educational institutions.

Today, our team consists of certified security professionals with expertise spanning offensive security, defensive operations, secure software development, and malware analysis. We remain committed to strengthening Indonesia's cybersecurity posture—one organization at a time.

Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our services, training programs, and how we work with clients.

We offer comprehensive penetration testing services including network infrastructure testing, web application security assessment, mobile application testing, API security testing, cloud configuration review, and social engineering simulations. Each engagement is tailored to your organization's specific needs and threat landscape.

All training programs are delivered live online via video conferencing platforms. Each session is interactive with hands-on lab exercises in controlled virtual environments. You will receive session recordings, course materials, and access to lab environments for practice between sessions.

Our programs are structured in three levels (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced). Beginner levels assume basic IT knowledge but no specific cybersecurity experience. Intermediate requires familiarity with basic concepts. Advanced levels expect solid foundational knowledge in the specific domain. Contact us if you are unsure which level fits your current skills.

Engagement duration depends on scope and complexity. A typical web application assessment takes 1-2 weeks. Network infrastructure testing may take 2-3 weeks. Full-scale red team exercises can run 4-8 weeks. We will provide a detailed timeline during the scoping phase based on your specific requirements.

Yes, upon completing any training program, you will receive a DevSec Indonesia Certificate of Completion. This certificate validates your participation and the skills you have acquired. While it is not a replacement for industry certifications like OSCP or CISSP, it demonstrates your commitment to professional development in cybersecurity.

All engagements are covered by a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). Testing data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Reports are delivered through secure channels. We follow strict data retention policies—client data is purged after the agreed retention period unless otherwise specified.
