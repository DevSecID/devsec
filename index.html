Company Profile

Protecting Indonesia's Digital Future

DevSec Indonesia is a dedicated cybersecurity company founded with a clear mission: to protect organizations and empower professionals through expert services and practical, hands-on training.

Our Vision

To become Indonesia's most trusted cybersecurity partner, enabling businesses and government institutions to operate securely in an increasingly complex digital landscape.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality security assessments, secure development consulting, and practical training programs that bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world cybersecurity challenges.

Our Story

2018 — Founded by a passionate security researcher.

2022 — Expanded to incident response, cloud security, red team.

2026 — 150+ projects, 500+ students trained.
